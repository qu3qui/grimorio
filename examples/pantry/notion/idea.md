# Idea — Pantry manager

**Status:** Pursue · **Captured:** 2026-10-02 · **Revisit on:** 2026-10-09

## Problem

I throw away food roughly every week because I forget what's in the pantry and fridge, and I buy duplicates because I don't remember what I already have.

## Why me / why now

I cook most days and shop on Saturdays. I want to learn Flutter with a local database, and this is a daily-use app I'd actually open.

## First rough solution

A phone app where I log what I buy with an expiry date, see what expires soon, and get a shopping list of what's running out.

## Existing alternatives

- Generic notes/to-do apps — no expiry dates or quantities.
- Dedicated pantry apps — heavy (accounts, barcode subscriptions, ads), more than I need.

## Scoring

| Criterion | 1–5 | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Value | 4 | Saves money and waste every week |
| Excitement | 4 | Daily use, visible results |
| Learning | 4 | Flutter + local DB + notifications |

## Decision

Pursue — high value, contained scope, good Flutter learning project.
